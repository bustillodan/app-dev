## Featured Series: *Stranger Things*

> "Friends don't lie." — **Eleven**

*Stranger Things* is a sci-fi horror drama series set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana.

### Quick Details

| Category | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Sci-Fi, Horror, Mystery, Drama |
| **Creators** | The Duffer Brothers |
| **Release Year** | `2016` |
| **My Rating** | 10/10 |

### Why It Is My Favorite
1. **Great Atmosphere:** Awesome 1980s soundtrack and nostalgic references.
2. **Memorable Characters:** Strong friendship and character growth across every season.
3. **Thrilling Plot:** Every season builds a deeper mystery around the *Upside Down*.

### Main Characters
* **Eleven:** Has telekinetic powers.
* **Mike Wheeler:** The loyal leader of the group.
* **Dustin Henderson:** The brains and comic relief of the team.
